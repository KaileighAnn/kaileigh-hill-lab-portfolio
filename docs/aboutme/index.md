# About Me 

Hi! I'm Kaileigh, a Mechanical Engineering student at UNC Charlotte concentrating in Precision Engineering and Metrology.

My interest in engineering started when I was pretty young. In middle school, I joined robotics clubs, which gave me some of my first experiences actually building things and figuring out how different parts worked together. I really enjoyed the hands-on side of it and being able to take an idea and turn it into something real.

When I got to high school, I continued that interest through an engineering program. That gave me the opportunity to learn more about the design process and different areas of engineering, and it ultimately helped me decide that Mechanical Engineering was what I wanted to pursue in college.

## Where I Am Now

Now, I'm continuing to build on those interests as a Mechanical Engineering student at UNC Charlotte. Through my classes and projects, I've gotten more experience with CAD, technical drawings, programming, circuits, manufacturing, and hands-on design.

I'm concentrating in **Precision Engineering and Metrology** because I'm interested in the more detailed side of engineering, especially how parts are designed, manufactured, and measured accurately. I'm excited to keep learning more about this area and figure out where I want to take it after graduation.

## A Little More About Me

Outside of engineering and school, I enjoy spending time with my friends and family, working out, traveling, and trying new things. I like staying busy, but I also enjoy having time to relax and spend time with the people who are important to me.

I think a lot of who I am outside of school carries over into engineering too. I'm naturally curious, I like figuring things out, and I'm always willing to learn something new.
